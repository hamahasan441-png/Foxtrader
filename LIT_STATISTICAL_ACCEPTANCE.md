# LiT statistical acceptance

Source correctness and CI success are necessary but not sufficient for a profitability claim.

Any LiT accuracy/win-rate statement must be measured on genuine out-of-sample data by symbol, timeframe, provider normalization, and market regime. Walk-forward splits must preserve chronology and must not tune thresholds on the evaluation window. Confidence scores are ranking/quality scores unless separately calibrated; they are not probabilities by default.

This repository intentionally makes no fixed or universal LiT win-rate claim.
